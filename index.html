      Sable Springs

      Three years ago, getaway driver Rio Vance left town at 120 miles an hour after a casino job went wrong. Now casino boss Silas Crane says Rio owes him $10,000, and cousin Tavi is paying for it. Rio is back for one night: take jobs, steal cars, outrun the cops, and take Crane down.

      Walk up to doors and press E: the Big Dice casino, Desert Arms gun shop, Lucky Pawn, your safehouse, and Crane's hangar at the airstrip north of town. Brown doors are apartments and houses (including the Paradise Palms suburb south of town): pick the lock, grab loot, sell it at the pawn shop. Documents give you dirt on VIPs, who then show a gold star. Press G next to them to blackmail. Clear a gang's turf with your crew to take it over for hourly income.

      West of the city runs the Sable River, fed by the Sable Dam reservoir. Speedboats wait at Riverside Marina, you can swim, cars sink, and the desert ramp near the river makes a great jump. Find the blue marker by the dam for the special mission High Water, or flood the city anytime from Custom mode.

      Gang turf: the Railyard Vipers (east) and Arroyo Kings (west) shoot on sight. Headshots do nearly three times the damage, and downed gang members drop cash and ammo.

      More ways to make money: hack blue ATMs, crack the Big Dice vault (gold ring), sell any car at the orange Ortega Auto marker, drive taxi fares or vigilante patrols, and win street races.

      Pink markers are story missions, gold markers are side jobs, green stacks are cash, orange crates are weapons, and pink crosses heal you. Rob registers at small green rings. Cops won't shoot while you hold a hostage. Progress saves in this browser.
